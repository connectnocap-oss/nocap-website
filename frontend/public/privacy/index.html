Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026 (analytics and WhatsApp added)

What we collect

When you send a brief, we collect the name, email address, brand and message you enter. We use them only to reply to you and discuss working together.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the site, for example which pages are viewed and whether a brief was sent. It uses cookies and collects device and usage information; we don't send your name, email or message to Google. You can block it with your browser's cookie settings or Google's opt-out add-on.

With a brief we also record which page you came from and any campaign tags in the link (for example utm_source), so we know which channels work.

WhatsApp

If you message us on WhatsApp, the conversation is handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own privacy policy.

How it's stored

Briefs are stored by our website backend and read by the NoCap team. We don't sell your information or share it with third parties for marketing.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the information you sent us, email connect.nocap@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
